WHO WE ARE

Gyanodaya Institute of Professional Studies

Gyanodaya Institute of Professional Studies (GIPS) is a premier teacher education institution committed to preparing skilled, ethical, and professional educators through quality academic programmes and practical learning.

The institution follows NCTE norms and focuses on innovation, educational research, leadership development, and modern teaching methodologies.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a leading institution in teacher education by promoting academic excellence, innovation, ethical values, and professional competence among future educators.
